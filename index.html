MONK-1

Twelve singers for the M-VAVE FM-1

MONK-1 puts MonkSynth's singing voice on your FM-1, with the twelve characters of its iOS port. They range from a monk to a slice of pizza, each on its own stage, and each mouth follows the vowel as it sings. Play harder for a louder note, and press into a held key to move the vowel. The FM-1's own keys do it, with no changes to the hardware.

Try it in the browserPlay it right here, with sound. It runs the same code as the FM-1. Click lower on a key for more velocity, and drag down for aftertouch. You can play it from the computer keyboard too. Install v0.10Connect your FM-1 by USB and install from Chrome or Edge. There's nothing to install on your computer. What it does

  • Pick from twelve singers: Jerry the monk, Guppo, Philip, Suze, Gerald, Bess, EeOo, Snider, Fipsy, Peppo, Marley and Razor. Each has its own voice and remembers your changes.
  • Play harder for a louder note. The FM-1's own keys read the velocity.
  • Press into a held key for aftertouch, to move the vowel, bend the pitch, or both. It counts from where the key comes to rest.
  • Shape the voice, MonkSynth's formant synthesis, four knobs at a time. Set the vowel, head size, breath and glide, add a choir of up to ten, and give it vibrato, echo and an envelope.
  • Use it over MIDI. It sends notes with velocity and polyphonic aftertouch over USB, and sings the notes, aftertouch and vowel it receives.
